Pairing sequence

  1. Switch the board into HID mode.
  2. Hold the left button for two seconds to open its 60-second BLE window.
  3. Select Connect and choose WITS Keyboard.
  4. Enter the six-digit PIN shown on the board when the system prompts.

Windows and Linux may require pairing in the operating system's Bluetooth settings first. Keep the board's pairing window open, enter the PIN shown on its display, then return here and select Connect.

Recovery: hold the right button for five seconds to clear saved board-side BLE bonds and reopen pairing.

Typing requires the red ARMED / READY screen. Only printable US keyboard characters, Tab, and newline/Enter are accepted in this phase.
